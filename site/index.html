Our approach

Clothing Made Like Your Favorite Tee, the One You Never Throw Out

Everyone owns one: the shirt that got better with every wash, fit like it was measured on you, and still gets compliments. Rogue&Co exists to make every piece that shirt.

We start with the fabric, not the trend. Heavyweight cotton for the graphics, denim that breaks in, and linen that breathes. Then we cut to real body measurements and print artist-drawn designs in small runs with water-based inks that move with the cotton instead of cracking.

The result is a smaller wardrobe that works harder: fewer returns, fewer replacements, and more days you actually like what you're wearing.

The usual tee

  • 140 GSM, see-through by summer
  • Plastisol print cracks after a few washes
  • One-size-fits-nobody grading
  • Same graphic as 40,000 other people

A Rogue&Co tee

  • 220 GSM combed cotton, pre-shrunk
  • Water-based inks that soften, not crack
  • Graded on real measurements, with a Fit Finder
  • 300 per print, then the design retires

The differenceUnlike fast-fashion basics, every Rogue&Co graphic is a numbered, limited run on fabric built for 100+ washes. You get the statement and the staple in one shirt.
